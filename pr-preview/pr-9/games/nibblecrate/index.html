Nibblecrate

  • Tap a crate to put it in the active row.
  • Crates in the row send their critters out to nibble pixels of their own colour. A critter can only reach a pixel that touches the open air around the picture.
  • A crate leaves the row once it has eaten its number. If the row is full and nobody can reach anything, you're stuck.
