Tanglecube

  • Tap an arrow to send it off the way its head points.
  • It slides straight along its face, so anything on that face in front of its head blocks it. Bodies can wrap round corners; heads can't.
  • Bonking into another arrow is free, unless you turn on hearts below.
  • Drag to turn the cube, pinch or scroll to zoom in close. Arrows on the back count too.
