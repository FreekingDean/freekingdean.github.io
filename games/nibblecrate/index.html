Nibblecrate

  • Tap a crate to let its critter out. It rides the belt once around the picture.
  • On the way it nibbles the first pixel it sees in each row and column, but only if that pixel is its own colour. The number is how hungry it still is.
  • Still hungry after a lap? It waits on the dock. Tap it to send it round again.
  • If a critter comes back and the dock is full, the belt jams. Eat the whole picture to win.
