    What to search

    Only needs your seed. A seed fixes which bag of 8 makes which item, and nothing you do in the run changes that (rerolling pedestals with a D6 doesn't either). This tries every bag of common-to-rare pickups and finds the easiest recipe for each game-breaking item and every quality 4: the one built from the most common pickups.

    Ranks the strongest items you can craft right now from the pickups above, then checks every pickup you could grab next and shows which one unlocks the most.

    Tiers: Breaker engines that snowball a run, S run-winners, A strong picks. The tier list is opinionated.

    How accurate is this?

    It runs the game's own crafting algorithm: the same seeded random number generator, item pools, pool weights and quality rules, ported from the reverse-engineered code used by the External Item Descriptions mod, with item data pulled straight from the game files.

    • Each version has its own link you can bookmark: Repentance+ (Switch 2) and Repentance (Switch 1).
    • Repentance+ uses the v1.9.7.15 files (the newest data dump available). Repentance+ came to Switch 2, PS5 and Xbox Series on 19 November 2026 and shares the PC version's content.
    • Repentance uses the v1.7.9b files. The Switch 1, PS4 and Xbox One versions were patched to 1.7.9b in April 2023, and they don't get Repentance+.
    • The two versions really do craft different things: Repentance+ changed some item qualities and item pools, and turned the 8-bomb recipe into Mr. Mega.
    • It assumes every item is unlocked. If the result needs an unlock you don't have, the bag rerolls, and the result card shows what you'd get instead.
